Questions owners ask us.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. On iPhone the order opens instantly from the camera as a lightweight App Clip. On Android it opens in the browser.

Will it ring if their phone is locked?

Yes. On iPhone the order lives on the lock screen and rings when you tap ready. On Android, customers allow alerts once when they open the page.

What do I need to start?

An iPad, tablet or computer with a browser, and internet. Sign up, add your logo, and open the cashier screen.

How does pay per order work?

Each order you page uses one point. Buy points in advance and we’ll alert you before they run out, or choose to be billed for your usage at the end of the month.

What happens after the free trial?

After 3 days, pick a plan to keep paging. Your settings and order history stay where they are.
